About us

We stay on site until the job is done.

We are civil contractors based in JP Nagar, Bengaluru. For over 15 years we have built apartments, independent houses, villas, commercial buildings and industrial sheds across the city and its outskirts, either turnkey or as a labour and material contract.

Every site has its own engineer and supervisor, and both report to the directors every day. We also handle the paperwork that holds most projects up: BBMP and BDA plan sanction, BESCOM and BWSSB connections, and the occupancy certificate.

Our process

  1. Site visit

    Our engineer visits your site anywhere in Bengaluru, checks the plot, khata and drawings, and listens to what you want to build. The visit is free.

  2. Estimate & BOQ

    You get an item-wise estimate with the materials spelt out, so you can compare quotes like for like.

  3. Agreement

    Scope, payment stages and timeline are written into the agreement before work begins.

  4. Construction

    We build in stages and send you photos and updates on WhatsApp every week, so you always know where things stand.

  5. Handover

    We walk the building with you, fix everything on the snag list, and help with the occupancy certificate and the BESCOM and BWSSB connections.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

The things people usually want to know before they start. Anything else, just call us on +91 98765 43210.

Do you offer a free site visit in Bengaluru?

Yes. One of our engineers visits your site anywhere in Bengaluru, checks the plot, khata and drawings, and talks you through the work and the costs. There is no charge for the visit.

What kind of construction work do you take up?

We build apartments from stilt + 4 up to B + G + 12, independent houses and villas, commercial buildings such as offices, showrooms and clinics, and PEB and RCC industrial sheds and warehouses. We also take up interiors, finishing work, renovation and structural repairs.

Do you take up turnkey construction projects?

Yes. You can give us the whole project on a turnkey basis, from excavation to handover, or as a labour and material contract.

How do you price a construction project?

Before work starts you get an item-wise BOQ (bill of quantities) with material specifications, so you can see the cost of every item and compare quotes properly. Scope, payment stages and the timeline are written into the agreement.

Can you help with BBMP and BDA plan approval?

Yes. We help with BBMP and BDA plan sanction paperwork, BESCOM and BWSSB connections, and the occupancy certificate at handover.

How will I know how my building is progressing?

Every site has a qualified site engineer and a supervisor who report to our directors every day. You also get progress photos and updates on WhatsApp every week.

Which areas of Bengaluru do you cover?

We work across Bengaluru and its outskirts, including Whitefield, Sarjapur Road, HSR Layout, Electronic City, Bannerghatta Road, JP Nagar, Kanakapura Road, Hebbal, Yelahanka, Devanahalli, Hoskote, Peenya, Bommasandra and Nelamangala.
