About us

A construction company that stays on site until the job is finished.

BMU is a civil contracting firm based in Bengaluru. We build apartments, independent houses, commercial buildings and industrial sheds across the city and its outskirts, either on a turnkey basis or as a labour and material contract.

Each site has a qualified site engineer and a supervisor who report to the directors every day. We also know the local process well: BBMP and BDA plan sanction, BESCOM and BWSSB connections, and the paperwork for the occupancy certificate.

How it works

From first call to final handover.

  1. Site visit
  2. Estimate & BOQ
  3. Agreement
  4. Construction
  5. Handover
0102030405

  1. Site visit

    Our engineer visits your site anywhere in Bengaluru, checks the plot, khata and drawings, and understands what you need. There is no charge for this.

  2. Estimate & BOQ

    You get an item-wise estimate with material specifications, so you can compare quotes properly.

  3. Agreement

    Scope, payment stages and timeline are written into the agreement before work begins.

  4. Construction

    Work is done stage by stage. You get regular site photos and progress updates on WhatsApp.

  5. Handover

    Final inspection with you, snag list closed, and support with the occupancy certificate and BESCOM and BWSSB connections.
